- No Lance build, test, lint, benchmark, generator, dependency installation or runtime performance qualification was executed. Source navigation and static reasoning are the evidence type.
- Historical verification/fixture records are retained as dated archives and confer no current coverage credit. Ordinary mapped files are a deliberate success under V2.1.
- Pointers are pinned to the recorded source commit. When upgrading Lance, recheck changed contracts and ranges; this report does not assert correctness for later revisions.
