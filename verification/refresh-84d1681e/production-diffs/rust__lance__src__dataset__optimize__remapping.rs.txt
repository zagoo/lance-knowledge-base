  689 +/// applied strictly in ledger order, so a compaction after a stable

  704 +/// hop. After the last hop an

  706 +/// replacement segment publishes: a row a partial compaction ceded to the
  707 +/// scan, a row a direct sibling took over, or a row in a fragment deleted
  708 +/// since is dropped even though its source was admitted. The remapped file
  709 +/// therefore carries no contribution its bitmap does not claim.
